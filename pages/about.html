About us

A new local IT business, built on word-of-mouth

Ke'Stone IT Support launched in 2026 after years of informal weekend call-outs for friends, family and neighbouring businesses. Those referrals turned into a steady stream of work — so we made it official, and we're now taking bookings online.

What drives us

Mission & vision

Our mission

To give small businesses and home users dependable, affordable IT support with honest advice and zero jargon — fixing the root cause, not just the symptom, and leaving every customer more confident with their own technology.

Our vision

To become the IT partner our community recommends by default: a trusted local team that keeps computers, networks and data safe so people can focus on the work that actually pays the bills.

The person behind it

Meet the founder

Kestone — Founder & Lead Technician

Kestone has spent years repairing machines, untangling home networks and rescuing data for people who were told their device was beyond saving. He started Ke'Stone IT Support because too many customers had been overcharged, talked down to, or handed back a laptop with the same problem it arrived with.

Every job is handled personally: a clear diagnosis, an upfront estimate, and a short explanation of what happened and how to avoid it next time.
